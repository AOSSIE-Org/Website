# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 1 November 2026

## Introduction

The AOSSIE Website at [aossie.org](https://aossie.org) (the Website) is committed to protecting your privacy and providing a transparent and user-friendly experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Website handles information when you visit it.

The Website follows a privacy-conscious and local-first approach. It is a static website: it has no user accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

## Information and Permissions

The Website does not ask you for personal information and does not request any device permissions.

The Website saves the following in your browser's local storage:

- `theme`: your light or dark mode choice

This stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

## Data Storage

The only thing the Website stores is your theme preference, which stays in your browser on your device and is not uploaded to or kept on any server. Request details that your browser sends when loading the Website are handled by GitHub, as described under Data Sharing.

## Data Sharing

The Website does not sell your personal information.

The Website does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

The Website relies on these third parties to work. Each one receives only what your browser sends to load the Website:

- **GitHub Pages** (hosting): GitHub receives your IP address and standard request details (such as your browser type and the page requested) whenever your browser loads the Website, and may keep them in server logs for security and operations. See [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **GitHub avatars** (contributor photos): your browser loads contributors' profile pictures from `github.com` and `avatars.githubusercontent.com`, so GitHub receives your IP address. See [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Links on the Website (GitHub, Discord, social media and project websites) take you to services with their own privacy policies.

The **Share** button in the footer opens the platform you choose (WhatsApp, X, LinkedIn, Telegram or your email app) with the address of the page you are on, or copies that address. That platform's own privacy policy applies to what you share there. The Website does not record what you share.

The **Support** page links to [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/AOSSIE-Org). If you choose to donate, you do so on GitHub, and GitHub's privacy statement applies. The Website does not receive any payment details.

Where a third party processes information, its handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy and terms of service.

## Data Security

The Website aims to minimize privacy and security risks by limiting unnecessary data collection and keeping your preferences on your device.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are also responsible for maintaining the security of their devices.

## Data Deletion

You can remove everything the Website stores by clearing your browser's site data for aossie.org.

## Children's Privacy

The Website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law. It does not collect personal information from any visitor.

## Free Access

The Website is free to use and has no paid features.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Website, its functionality, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be made available wherever this Privacy Policy is published.
